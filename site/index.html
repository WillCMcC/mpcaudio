MPC USB Audio

MPC One / One+ · two-way audio over USB in standalone

  1. On the MPC: Menu › Preferences › Info › Update › Switch to Update Mode.
  2. Connect the MPC’s USB-B port to this computer.

  3. Installs MPC 3.9.1 (latest) with two-way USB audio — record the MPC’s main out on the computer, and play computer audio into the MPC’s inputs 1/2 — plus SSH (root, no password).
